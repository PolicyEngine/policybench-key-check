## Part C. Statute as amended (26 U.S.C.), fetched 2026-11-10

Saved under `sources/uscode/` as `usc26-s<N>.html` (raw, from `https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s<N>/`) and `usc26-s<N>.txt` (tags stripped). Each page says "Text contains those laws in effect through Public Law 119-138 (November 3, 2026)" (e.g. usc26-s63.txt:27). Also `lii-26-24.html/.txt` (Cornell LII copy of § 24; same operative text).

**Finding C0 (later laws).** Of the 22 sections fetched (1, 21, 22, 24, 25B, 32, 55, 62, 63, 68, 151, 163, 164, 170, 199A, 219, 221, 224, 225, 1401, 1411, 3101), only three cite a 119th-Congress law other than P.L. 119-21, and none changes a dollar amount, rate or threshold in this table:
- P.L. 119-108 (Sept. 11, 2026, "Doug LaMalfa Federal Disaster Tax Relief Certainty Act") added § 63(b)(8): non-itemizers may deduct the qualified net disaster loss; effective for years after 2024 (usc26-s63.txt:42, :126, :227-229). No board input carries a disaster loss (inputs-inventory.tsv), so this does not bear on the board (inference from the inventory).
- P.L. 119-111 (Sept. 18, 2026) extended the § 62(a)(2)(D) educator expense to early childhood teachers; the dollar limit is untouched (usc26-s62.txt:166-168, :244).
- § 1 cites P.L. 119-125 (Clergy Act, note under § 1402), 119-108, 119-64, 119-39, 119-29 only in short-title notes (usc26-s1.txt:815-824); none amends § 1's text.
- Sections 22, 32, 219, 221, 1401, 1411, 3101 cite no 119th-Congress law at all.
So for every row below, the law is P.L. 119-21 as codified, plus the two revenue procedures.
