## C1. SALT: what PolicyEngine deducts for state income or sales tax

- F-C1.1 PE does not deduct the state income tax it computes. `state_and_local_sales_or_income_tax` = max(state_withheld_income_tax + local_income_tax, state_sales_tax + local_sales_tax) (PEU/variables/gov/irs/income/taxable_income/deductions/itemizing/state_and_local_sales_or_income_tax.py:11-17). `state_withheld_income_tax` (TaxUnit) `adds` the 43 person-level `<st>_withheld_income_tax` variables listed in PEU/parameters/gov/states/household/state_withheld_income_tax.yaml:3-54 (PEU/variables/gov/states/tax/income/state_withheld_income_tax.py:4-10).
- F-C1.2 Each `<st>_withheld_income_tax` is a per-PERSON withholding proxy: that person's `adjusted_gross_income_person` minus a fixed single-filer deduction, run through the state's SINGLE rate schedule, whatever the household's filing status. Read: CA = AGI_person - CA std ded SINGLE, rates.single (ca_withheld_income_tax.py:12-17); NY same with NY std ded SINGLE, main.single (ny_withheld_income_tax.py:12-18); IL = rate x (AGI_person - personal exemption) (il_withheld_income_tax.py:12-19); WI = AGI_person - WI std ded max SINGLE, rates.single (wi_withheld_income_tax.py:12-18); ID and MO subtract the FEDERAL std ded SINGLE (id_withheld_income_tax.py:14-19; mo_withheld_income_tax.py:14-19); MN subtracts MN std ded base SINGLE (mn_withheld_income_tax.py:13-18); MI = rate x max(AGI_person,0), no deduction (mi_withheld_income_tax.py:12-19); MD = AGI_person - MD std ded max SINGLE through MD single rates PLUS a county tax at the county flat rate, defaulting to ALLEGANY_COUNTY_MD when county is UNKNOWN (md_withheld_income_tax.py:12-47). Credits, other deductions, exemptions (except IL), joint brackets and refundable credits play no part. Another model that deducts actual state liability (TAXSIM computes state tax; Tax-Calculator takes e18400 as given) will differ for every itemizer with state income tax.
- F-C1.3 Consequence for the convention modules: a state parameter that a withholding proxy reads moves the federal SALT deduction and so `income_tax_before_refundable_credits` for itemizers in that state. ## C2. Itemize or not

- F-C2.1 `gov.simulation.branch_to_determine_itemization` = true (PEU/parameters/gov/simulation/branch_to_determine_itemization.yaml:3). `tax_unit_itemizes` then compares FEDERAL `income_tax` (after refundable credits) computed in two branches with tax_unit_itemizes forced True and False (tax_unit_itemizes.py:66-87; tax_liability_if_itemizing.py:11-13; tax_liability_if_not_itemizing.py:12-14; PEU/tools/general.py:188-201). It itemizes when that tax is lower by more than $0.01; when the two taxes tie within $0.01 it itemizes iff itemized deductions > standard deduction + charitable_deduction_for_non_itemizers, where for 2026 (limitation.applies and obbb.applies both true) the itemized amount is `itemized_taxable_income_deductions` from the itemizing branch (tax_unit_itemizes.py:43-58, 72-87). ## Rules another tax model is likely to implement differently, or cannot take as an input

1. SALT state income tax is NOT the household's state liability: it is a per-person withholding proxy (each person's AGI less a SINGLE standard deduction, through the SINGLE schedule; ID and MO use the federal SINGLE standard deduction; MD adds county tax; local income tax and 20%-of-state local sales tax added) and the larger of that or the IRS optional sales-tax table amount is used (F-C1). Convention modules move it in CA, WI, ID, MN, MD, MO and the sales table everywhere (C5).
2. $500 credit for other dependents goes to EVERY non-head, non-spouse member without a qualifying-child CTC, with no 152(d) gross-income or support test; adult dependentN members (064, 067, 093) get it whatever their income (F-C4.3, F-V1).
3. Dependency/qualifying-child tests are age-only: no relationship, residency or support test; K-12 student status is imputed for ages 5-17 only; is_disabled never counts as permanent and total disability, so disabled adult dependents fail the CTC/EITC/HOH age exceptions (F-A1.6 to F-A1.8, F-C4.8).
4. Zero by construction on the board, though a model reading the prompt may claim them: tip deduction (needs a Treasury tipped-occupation code, an int input the board cannot state), car-loan interest deduction (auto_loan_interest has no reader), AOC/LLC (no tuition input), self-employed health insurance ALD (board premiums use a name the ALD does not read), domestic production deduction (repealed list), unreimbursed employee expenses (misc deduction off) (F-C3.6, F-C3.8, F-C4.12, F-B2b, F-B3, F-B4).
5. PE adds a modelled Medicare Part B premium (2,434.80 in 2026, plus IRMAA, less MSP) for every 65+ person to itemized medical expenses, although the board input is "premiums without Medicare Part B" (F-B2a). Matters for 65+ itemizers (072, 114).
6. Alimony paid is always deductible (divorce_year defaults to 2010; the board cannot state an int) (F-B4). HSA ALD is taken as stated with no contribution cap (F-B4).
7. Itemization is chosen by comparing federal tax under both branches, not by comparing deduction totals (F-C2); the senior deduction applies in both branches and phases out PER PERSON (F-C3.3); the charitable deduction has the 2026 0.5%-of-AGI floor for itemizers and a 1,000/2,000 cash-only deduction for non-itemizers (F-C3.4, F-C3.5).
8. income_tax_before_refundable_credits includes NIIT, the 10% early-distribution penalty (qualified_retirement_penalty), unreported payroll tax and investment-credit recapture; nonrefundable credits are capped at regular tax + capital-gains tax + AMT only (F-C4.0).
9. Mortgage interest: deductible share = 750,000 / total balance when balances exceed 750,000 (all loans treated as post-2017; origination year cannot be stated); with no balance stated, interest is fully deductible (F-B1).
10. CDCC: the whole stated SPM childcare amount (care of 13-17-year-olds included) counts up to 3,000 x min(children under 13, 2), minus any PE-modelled state subsidy, at the 2026 50%/35%/20% schedule; an earner-less head zeroes it (117) (F-C4.9 to F-C4.11, F-V1).
11. QBI deduction floor of 400 for >= 1,000 of QBI from 2026 with no material-participation test (F-C3.9).
12. No board household is SURVIVING_SPOUSE or SEPARATE: 12 heads state is_surviving_spouse, but PE requires a child dependent and 11 of the 12 live alone (F-A1.4, F-V1).
