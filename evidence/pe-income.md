## B. The four outputs and how they aggregate (structure every input entry below relies on)

- B1. federal_income_tax_before_refundable_credits = `income_tax_before_refundable_credits` (tax unit) = income_tax_before_credits + net_investment_income_tax + recapture_of_investment_credit + unreported_payroll_tax + qualified_retirement_penalty - income_tax_capped_non_refundable_credits (`gov/irs/tax/federal_income/income_tax_before_refundable_credits.py:18-32`); income_tax_before_credits = income_tax_main_rates + capital_gains_tax + alternative_minimum_tax (`.../before_credits/income_tax_before_credits.py:12-16`). So NIIT IS in this line; Additional Medicare Tax and SE tax are NOT. recapture_of_investment_credit, unreported_payroll_tax and qualified_retirement_penalty are bare inputs with no formula (`gov/irs/credits/recapture_of_investment_credit.py`, `gov/irs/tax/payroll/unreported_payroll_tax.py`, `household/expense/tax/qualified_retirement_penalty.py`, each 9 lines): the board states none, so 0. PE does NOT compute the sec. 72(t) 10% additional tax on early retirement distributions from age.
- B2. federal_refundable_credits = `income_tax_refundable_credits` = adds `gov.irs.credits.refundable` (`gov/irs/credits/income_tax_refundable_credits.py:10`); list from 2022-01-01: eitc, refundable_american_opportunity_credit, refundable_ctc, recovery_rebate_credit, refundable_payroll_tax_credit; the premium tax credit is not in it (`parameters/gov/irs/credits/refundable.yaml:20-26`).
- B3. self_employment_tax: person-level `self_employment_tax` = self_employment_social_security_tax + self_employment_medicare_tax (`gov/irs/tax/self_employment/self_employment_tax.py:14-21`); the board's spec has aggregation "sum" and `_extract_scalar_value` sums the person array (`PB/policybench/benchmark_specs.json:42-51`, `PB/policybench/ground_truth.py:68-77`), so EVERY person's SE tax counts, dependents and children included. No dependent filter anywhere in the SE-tax chain.
- B4. payroll_tax = `spm_unit_payroll_tax` = sum over contained tax units of `employee_payroll_tax` (`household/expense/tax/spm_unit_payroll_tax.py:11-12`, spec `benchmark_specs.json:31-40`); employee_payroll_tax = add(tax_unit, [employee_social_security_tax, employee_medicare_tax, additional_medicare_tax, employee_state_payroll_tax]) (`gov/irs/tax/payroll/employee_payroll_tax.py:3-22`). `add` over a tax unit of a person variable sums every member, so employee SS and Medicare tax of a dependent or child COUNT; additional_medicare_tax is tax-unit level and counts head+spouse only (B7).
- B5. Wage bases: `payroll_tax_gross_wages` = max(0, employment_income - fica_pre_tax_contributions) (`gov/irs/tax/payroll/payroll_tax_gross_wages.py:13-16`); fica_pre_tax_contributions adds `gov.irs.gross_income.fica_pre_tax_contributions` = [pre_tax_health_insurance_premiums, health_savings_account_payroll_contributions] (`parameters/gov/irs/gross_income/fica_pre_tax_contributions.yaml:3-5`) — 401(k)/403(b) deferrals do NOT reduce FICA wages. `irs_employment_income` (income-tax wages, W-2 box 1) = max(0, employment_income - pre_tax_contributions) (`.../irs_gross_income/irs_employment_income.py:12-15`), pre_tax_contributions = [traditional_401k_contributions, traditional_403b_contributions, pre_tax_health_insurance_premiums, health_savings_account_payroll_contributions] (`parameters/gov/irs/gross_income/pre_tax_contributions.yaml:3-12`); roth_401k is not in it.
- B6. `taxable_earnings_for_social_security` = min(cap, payroll_tax_gross_wages) (`gov/irs/tax/payroll/social_security/taxable_earnings_for_social_security.py:11-13`); employee_social_security_tax = rate.employee x that (`.../employee_social_security_tax.py:12-14`); employee_medicare_tax = rate.employee x payroll_tax_gross_wages, uncapped (`.../medicare/employee_medicare_tax.py:12-14`). All person-level, no dependent filter. The cap is per person (one employer assumed); no excess-SS-withholding refund enters the four outputs (excess_payroll_tax_withheld is a separate variable, not in B1-B4 lists).
- B7. `additional_medicare_tax` (tax unit, `gov/irs/tax/federal_income/additional_medicare_tax.py:22-29`) = rate x max(0, tax_unit_non_dep_add([payroll_tax_gross_wages, taxable_self_employment_income]) - exclusion[filing_status]): head + spouse only (`tax_unit_non_dep_add`, `tools/general.py:89-119`), wages and SE income pooled against ONE threshold. Because taxable_self_employment_income is never negative (B9), this equals Form 8959 Parts I+II for every sign case (my algebra: max(0,W-T) + max(0,S-max(0,T-W)) = max(0,W+S-T) for S>=0). additional_medicare_tax_withheld is a separate person variable (`.../medicare/additional_medicare_tax_withheld.py`) and not in the payroll output.
- B8. SE income base: `taxable_self_employment_income` (person, `gov/irs/tax/self_employment/taxable_self_employment_income.py:12-32`) = (self_employment_income + sstb_self_employment_income + farm_operations_income + partnership_self_employment_net_earnings) x (1 - employer_share 0.5 x (0.124 + 0.029)) = x 0.9235, set to 0 when that product < net_earnings_exemption ($400) (`parameters/gov/irs/self_employment/net_earnings_exemption.yaml`, rates `parameters/gov/irs/self_employment/rate/*.yaml`: 0.124 and 0.029 from 2013). The four sources are netted WITHIN a person before the 0.9235 factor (a Schedule C loss offsets a Schedule F or K-1 profit of the same person); never across spouses. NOT in the base: partnership_s_corp_income, farm_rent_income, farm_income, rental_income.
- B9. Wage-base ordering: `social_security_taxable_self_employment_income` = min(taxable_self_employment_income, cap - taxable_earnings_for_social_security) (`.../social_security_taxable_self_employment_income.py:12-23`): wages use the Social Security wage base first, SE income gets the remainder (Schedule SE lines 7-9 order), per person. SE Medicare = 2.9% x taxable_self_employment_income with no cap (`.../self_employment_medicare_tax.py:12-14`). Because the result of the `where` at :32 is 0 or >= 400, taxable_self_employment_income >= 0 always.
- B10. SE tax deduction: `self_employment_tax_ald_person` = self_employment_tax x `gov.irs.ald.self_employment_tax.percent_deductible` (`.../above_the_line_deductions/self_employment_tax_ald_person.py:13-18`); `self_employment_tax_ald` (tax unit) sums it over non-dependents only (`.../self_employment_tax_ald.py:21-24`). A dependent's SE tax is in the SE-tax output (B3) but its deduction is not on the filer's return.

## C. Earned income definitions (three different ones)

- C1. EITC and refundable CTC: `eitc_earned_income` (tax unit, `gov/irs/credits/earned_income/eitc_earned_income.py:19-42`) = max(0, sum over NON-DEPENDENTS (`tax_unit_non_dep_sum`, `tools/general.py:82-86`) of irs_employment_income + self_employment_income + sstb_self_employment_income + farm_operations_income + partnership_self_employment_net_earnings + earned_disability_benefits, minus the non-dependents' self_employment_tax_ald_person). Sources are summed signed (a Schedule C/F loss lowers it) before the single zero floor. Uses irs_employment_income, i.e. wages AFTER traditional 401(k)/403(b) deferrals. Readers: `eitc_phased_in.py:15`, `eitc_reduction.py:13` (phase-out on max(eitc_earned_income, AGI), :13-19), and the ACTC phase-in `ctc/refundable/ctc_phase_in_relevant_earnings.py:23-25` (max(0, earnings - refundable.phase_in.threshold) x rate).
- C2. CDCC: `head_earned` / `spouse_earned` (`gov/irs/credits/cdcc/head_earned.py:11-15`, `spouse_earned.py:11-15`) read person `adjusted_earnings` = max(0, employment_income + self_employment_income + sstb_self_employment_income + earned_disability_benefits - 0.5 x self_employment_tax) (`gov/irs/income/adjusted_earnings.py:11-21`, earned_income adds at `.../earned_income/earned_income.py:12-16`; self_emp_tax_adj = 0, employer_share = 0.5). DIFFERENT from C1: it uses GROSS employment_income (before 401(k)/403(b) deferrals and pre-tax premiums), and it leaves out farm_operations_income and partnership_self_employment_net_earnings. `min_head_spouse_earned` = head_earned unless joint, else min(head, spouse) with the 21(d)(2) deemed floor (`cdcc/min_head_spouse_earned.py:12-47`).
- C3. `tax_unit_earned_income` = sum over non-dependents of earned_income (`.../earned_income/tax_unit_earned_income.py:11`) is read in the federal code only by `basic_standard_deduction.py:31` (the dependent-filer standard deduction limit). `filer_adjusted_earnings` (`gov/irs/income/filer_adjusted_earnings.py`) has no federal reader (grep of `variables` outside `/states/`).
- C4. EITC investment-income test: `eitc_relevant_investment_income` (`gov/irs/credits/earned_income/eitc_relevant_investment_income.py:49-117`), head+spouse only = taxable_interest_income + tax_exempt_interest_income + dividend_income (portfolio, unfloored) + max(0, LTCG + STCG + max(0, non_sch_d_capital_gains)) + max(0, rental_income + farm_rent_income + passive_partnership_s_corp_income + passive_estate_income - eitc_passive_income_also_in_earned_income). passive_partnership_s_corp_income, passive_estate_income and eitc_passive_income_also_in_earned_income are inputs defaulting to 0 (`variables/input/passive_partnership_s_corp_income.py:15`, `household/income/person/estate/passive_estate_income.py:28`, `gov/irs/credits/earned_income/eitc_passive_income_also_in_earned_income.py:31`), so for the board partnership_s_corp_income and estate_income are NOT investment income, while ALL rental_income and farm_rent_income are. Threshold check `eligibility/eitc_investment_income_eligible.py:13-14` (<= eitc.phase_out.max_investment_income). Note `parameters/gov/irs/gross_income/disqualified_earned_income.yaml` (dividend, tax-exempt interest, interest, rental, capital_gains) is NOT what the EITC formula reads.

## E. Qualified business income deduction (QBID) machinery

- E1. Per-person `qbid_amount` (`gov/irs/income/taxable_income/deductions/qualified_business_income_deduction/qbid_amount.py:19-179`): non-SSTB QBI and SSTB QBI computed separately; SSTB QBI x applicable_rate (1 - min(1, (taxable_income_less_qbid - phase_out.start)/phase_out.length)) (:27-44, :141); losses netted across head+spouse proportionally to positive QBI, a dependent netted alone (:146-159); each positive component limited by the wage/UBIA cap max(0.5 x W2, 0.25 x W2 + 0.025 x UBIA) phased in over the range (:48-57, :126-132); plus 0.2 x max(0, qualified_reit_and_ptp_income) with no cap (:172-176). Rates: max.rate (0.2 from 2018 per file history), reit_ptp_rate 0.2, w2 rate 0.5, alt 0.25, property 0.025 (`parameters/gov/irs/deductions/qbi/max/...`).
- E2. QBI sources: `parameters/gov/irs/deductions/qbi/income_definition.yaml:3-9` = self_employment_income, partnership_s_corp_income, farm_rent_income, farm_operations_income, rental_income, estate_income, each x `<var>_would_be_qualified`. Defaults: True for self_employment_income, partnership_s_corp_income, farm_rent_income, farm_operations_income, rental_income; FALSE for estate_income (`household/income/person/estate/estate_income_would_be_qualified.py:21`). SSTB: sstb_self_employment_income x sstb_self_employment_income_would_be_qualified (default True). `partnership_self_employment_net_earnings` is NOT a QBI source. QBI is reduced by a pro-rata share of self_employment_tax_ald_person + self_employed_health_insurance_ald_person + self_employed_pension_contribution_ald_person (`deduction_definition.yaml:3-6`; `qualified_business_income.py:19-42`, `sstb_qualified_business_income.py:21-44`). `qualified_business_income` is `defined_for = business_is_qualified` (default True, `household/.../business_is_qualified.py:15`); `business_is_sstb` default False; `w2_wages_from_qualified_business` and `sstb_w2_wages_from_qualified_business` default 0 (board states neither).
- E3. Tax-unit `qualified_business_income_deduction` (`.../qualified_business_income_deduction.py:18-58`): sum of filers' qbid_amount, capped at 0.2 x max(0, taxable_income_less_qbid - section_199a_net_capital_gain) (:25-36). From 2026 (`parameters/gov/irs/deductions/qbi/deduction_floor/in_effect.yaml`: true from 2026-01-01) the result is max(capped amount, floor) where floor = $400 when the filers' total QBI (non-SSTB + SSTB x applicable_rate, signed, after the SE-related QBI deductions) >= $1,000 (`deduction_floor/amount.yaml`: 0 below 1,000, 400 at or above) (:37-57). The floor is applied AFTER the 20%-of-taxable-income cap and has no material-participation test, so rental_income and farm_rent_income (QBI by default) can trigger it.
- E4. `section_199a_net_capital_gain` (`.../section_199a_net_capital_gain.py:48-77`) = max(0, min(LTCG + non_sch_d_capital_gains, that + STCG)) + max(0, QDI), head+spouse only.
- E5. UBIA inputs only reach `qbid_amount`'s cap; with W-2 wages 0 the cap is 0.025 x UBIA, and it binds only when taxable_income_less_qbid exceeds phase_out.start (below it line 12 = full 20%, :53-57). `sstb_unadjusted_basis_qualified_property` is read ONLY when the person has both SSTB and non-SSTB amounts (`has_mixed_categories`, :80-82, :107-124); with only one category the person's `unadjusted_basis_qualified_property` total is used for that category. Board scenario_114 (head: sstb UBIA 35,351.48, partnership_s_corp_income 24,361.77, no SSTB income, no plain UBIA): the SSTB UBIA is never read and the non-SSTB UBIA is 0.

## G. Business, rental, farm, estate income and losses

- G1. Schedule C/F/E inputs are pure inputs: `farm_operations_income` ("Schedule F. A map that sends both to one Schedule F field double-counts.
- G4. Losses: gross income floors each source at 0 per person (A4); losses come back through `loss_ald` = limited_business_loss + capital_losses_allowed_against_gains + limited_capital_loss (`above_the_line_deductions/loss_ald.py:22-26`). `limited_business_loss` (`limited_business_loss.py:21-100`): head+spouse losses from self_employment_income and sstb_self_employment_income (each category separately), farm_operations_income, rental_income, farm_rent_income, estate_income, partnership_s_corp_income, other_net_gain, capped at their business income + `gov.irs.ald.loss.max[filing_status]` (sec. 461(l); 2026: 256,000 single/separate, 512,000 joint per `parameters/gov/irs/ald/loss/max.yaml`). NO sec. 469 passive-activity limit and no $25,000 rental allowance anywhere in this chain: a rental, farm-rent, estate or partnership loss offsets wages in full. Board examples: scenario_098 farm_rent_income -11,100; scenario_120 farm_rent -2,446.59 with rental +2,000; scenario_004 partnership -135; scenario_033 SSTB SE -11,400; scenario_039 SE -6,260 with estate +25,950; scenario_042 farm ops -1,127; scenario_064 farm ops -16,800.
- G5. A Schedule C/F loss also enters the SE base signed (B8: netted within the person before x0.9235), so scenario_064's head SE base is (14,000 - 16,800) x 0.9235 < 0 -> 0 SE tax, and EITC earned income (C1) is reduced by it, while CDCC earned income (C2) ignores farm and partnership SE amounts.

## H. Capital gains

- H1. `capital_gains` (person) = STCG + LTCG (`household/income/person/capital_gains/capital_gains.py:11`); gross income takes max(0, capital_gains) per person plus max(0, non_sch_d_capital_gains) per person (A4/A5). `capital_losses` = max(0, -capital_gains) per person (`capital_gains/capital_losses.py:12-13`). `capital_losses_allowed_against_gains` = min(filers' capital_losses, filers' floored gains incl. distributions) (`above_the_line_deductions/capital_losses_allowed_against_gains.py:38-60`); `limited_capital_loss` = min(3,000 / 1,500 MFS, excess) (`limited_capital_loss.py:28-43`, `parameters/gov/irs/ald/loss/capital/max.yaml`). Net effect: spouses' gains and losses net on a joint return; no capital loss carryover input exists.
- H2. Preferential rate base: `filer_schedule_d_lines` (`gov/irs/tax/federal_income/capital_gains/filer_schedule_d_lines.py`) line 7 = filers' STCG, line 15 = filers' LTCG + max(0, non_sch_d_capital_gains) per filer; `net_capital_gain` = max(0, line15 - max(0, -line7)) + max(0, filers' QDI) (minus Form 4952 elections, none stated) (`net_capital_gain.py:47-78`); adjusted_net_capital_gain removes unrecaptured 1250 and 28% gain (none stated) (`adjusted_net_capital_gain.py:20-51`); `capital_gains_tax` applies cg.rates 1-3 over cg.thresholds by filing status (`capital_gains_tax.py:1-88`), and `capital_gains_excluded_from_taxable_income` removes the preferential part from main-rate income (`capital_gains_excluded_from_taxable_income.py`). non_sch_d_capital_gains is long-term (852(b)(3)(B)) in this computation. Dependents' gains and QDI are excluded everywhere (tax_unit_non_dep_add).
- H3. long_term_capital_gains is set directly by the board (A2); `capital_gains_behavioral_response` is bypassed. STCG and non_sch_d_capital_gains are plain inputs (`short_term_capital_gains.py`, `non_sch_d_capital_gains.py`); a negative non_sch_d_capital_gains is treated as 0 everywhere (its documentation, :9-18).

## J. Retirement contributions (`_desired` inputs) and the saver's credit

- J1. 401(k): `traditional_401k_contributions` = traditional_401k_contributions_desired x `elective_deferral_contribution_scale` (`household/expense/retirement/traditional_401k_contributions.py:15-18`); roth_401k likewise (`roth_401k_contributions.py:15-18`). - J2. - J3. IRAs: traditional_ira_contributions and roth_ira_contributions = desired x `ira_contribution_scale` = min(1, ira_contribution_limit / combined desired) (`traditional_ira_contributions.py:16-19`, `roth_ira_contributions.py:18-21`, `ira_contribution_scale.py:18-35`). `ira_contribution_limit` = min(dollar limit + age catch-up, compensation, or the spousal compensation for the lower earner on a joint return) (`ira_contribution_limit.py:19-74`). `ira_compensation` = max(0, irs_employment_income + earned_disability_benefits) + max(0, taxable_alimony_income) + max(0, SE + SSTB SE + farm ops + partnership SE - SE tax ALD - SE pension ALD) (`ira_compensation.py:19-40`). - J4. The lead skipped `traditional_ira_contributions_ald` (no such variable in 2.60.0; meta.json `skipped_variables`).
- J5. Saver's credit (nonrefundable, in the board's federal_income_tax_before_refundable_credits): per person `savers_credit_qualified_contributions` = max(0, sum of traditional_ira, roth_ira, traditional_401k, traditional_403b, roth_401k, roth_403b, self_employed_pension contributions (2026 list, `parameters/gov/irs/credits/retirement_saving/qualified_retirement_savings_contributions.yaml:13-20`) - that person's own current-year `retirement_distributions`) (`gov/irs/credits/retirement_savings/savers_credit_qualified_contributions.py:1-7`); retirement_distributions = taxable + tax-exempt IRA, 401(k), SEP, 403(b), keogh distributions (`household/income/person/retirement/retirement_distributions.py`, `tax_exempt_retirement_distributions.py`) — NOT pensions (taxable_private_pension_income / tax_exempt_private_pension_income are not in it). Only the person's own distributions, current year only (no prior-two-year look-back, no spouse's distributions; law reading of 25B(d)(2) not checked this run). ## N. Whose amounts count (summary)

- Income tax line (B1) and refundable credits (B2): head and spouse only. Every gross income source is zeroed for `is_tax_unit_dependent` persons (A4); ALDs sum non-dependents (A4, B10); EITC earned and investment income, CTC Social Security taxes, capital gains (Schedule D lines), NIIT, QBID filers and IRA deduction use head/spouse (`tax_unit_non_dep_sum` / `tax_unit_non_dep_add`, `tools/general.py:82-119`). The board's to_pe_household puts everyone in ONE tax unit, and PE marks adults beyond head/spouse (dependentN/adultN) and children as tax-unit dependents (inference from `is_tax_unit_dependent` = not head and not spouse; brief-pe-income's household description; not re-probed). A dependent's own income tax is therefore never computed: their wages, disability benefits etc. simply drop out of the unit's income tax.
- Exceptions that sum ALL members: tip and overtime deductions (F1, F2), unemployment taxable allocation (I4), sales-tax-table income for itemizers (K2), nontaxable pensions in the elderly credit (L2), `qualifies_for_elderly_or_disabled_credit` count (L2, :18-20).
- self_employment_tax output (B3): every person, dependents and children included; the dependent's SE-tax ALD is NOT taken by the filer (B10).
- payroll_tax output (B4): every person's employee SS and Medicare tax; Additional Medicare head+spouse only (B7).
- Board persons with income who are dependents: scenario_123 child1 age 16 wages 45,000; scenario_093 dependent1 age 27 wages 45,000; scenario_067 dependent1 age 23 disability_benefits 10,800; scenario_064 dependent1 age 27 traditional_401k_desired (385.90 actual, no wages). Their wages carry payroll tax in the payroll output but no income tax anywhere in the four outputs.

## O. Surprising inputs / ones another model likely treats differently

1. partnership_se_income (partnership_self_employment_net_earnings) is NOT income in PE: SE base, EITC earned income and IRA compensation only (G2). scenario_064 pairs it (14,000) with partnership_s_corp_income (19,350); a model that adds both to AGI counts 14,000 more.
2. farm_income has no federal effect and equals farm_operations_income wherever it appears (scenario_042, 064) (G3).
3. tip_income has no effect (inside wages; no tipped-occupation code, deduction 0) (F1). fsla_overtime_premium is inside wages; its deduction ignores FLSA status and sums all members (F2).
4. Dependents' and children's income is outside income tax but their payroll and SE tax are inside the board's payroll/SE outputs (N).
5. qualified_bdc_income: no effect (D2); qualified_reit_and_ptp_income: QBID-only, not income (D3).
6. QBI includes rental_income and farm_rent_income by default, excludes estate_income; 2026 $400 minimum QBID applies after the taxable-income cap with no material-participation test (E2, E3).
7. No passive-activity (sec. 469) limit: rental, farm-rent, estate and partnership losses offset wages in full up to sec. 461(l) (G4).
8. Three earned-income definitions: EITC/ACTC use post-401(k) wages incl. farm ops and partnership SE; CDCC uses gross wages and omits them; ACTC 3+ children alternative uses payroll on gross wages (C1, C2, L1).
9. salt_refund_income fully taxable (no tax-benefit test, K11); disability_benefits fully taxable and not earned income (K4); alimony taxable via default divorce_year 2010 (K8); survivor_benefits, veterans_benefits, workers_compensation, child support, educational and financial assistance untaxed (K5-K9).
10. 401(k) deferrals cut income-tax wages but not FICA wages; desired deferrals are capped by 402(g)+catch-up but not by wages; IRA contributions (traditional and Roth) are capped at compensation, so a no-earnings person gets 0 and no saver's credit from them (J1-J3).
11. NIIT sits inside the income-tax-before-refundable-credits line; Additional Medicare Tax sits in the payroll line (B1, B4). farm_rent_income is not NII (K13).
12. tax_exempt_interest_income, veterans_benefits, workers_compensation, tax-exempt pensions/IRA distributions and untaxed SS enter federal income tax for itemizers through the IRS sales-tax-table income bracket (K2); for non-itemizers they have no effect (except tax-exempt interest via provisional income and EITC investment income).
